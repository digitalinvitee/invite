Jetour

A private drive from Tbilisi to Orbeti, announced by a bilingual invitation that behaves like the car's own display: a timed intro film, a live countdown, the agenda and a two-path RSVP.

Client
JETOUR Georgia
Occasion
New-model presentation and evening, 28 June 2026
Place
Satibe, Orbeti
Languages
Georgian (default), English
Scope
Invitation website, intro sequence, countdown, agenda, RSVP and guest data
Live site
digitalinvitee.github.io/JETOUR ↗ (opens in a new tab)

(01)Challenge

Invite guests to a drive, not a dinner.

JETOUR Georgia presented a new model at Satibe in Orbeti, outside Tbilisi: welcome drinks at 18:00, the presentation at 19:00, then dinner, music and the sunset. The invitation had to set that tone before anyone got in a car, carry the practical details of an out-of-town evening and collect replies with guest numbers, in Georgian first.

(02)Concept

An invitation that behaves like the car's own interface.

Dark surfaces, figures in a dashboard typeface and a copper accent that matches the car on screen. The guest starts the engine, reads the instruments, then takes the route.

  1. 01 · Ignition

    904 hp

    A vertical film of the new model plays first, with three figures timed to its cuts. Music waits for the guest's first tap.

  2. 02 · Instruments

    28.06.2026 · 18:00

    The hero reads like a cluster: date, time and route, then a live countdown to the welcome drink.

  3. 03 · Route

    Tbilisi → Orbeti

    Agenda, dress code and a playlist for the drive, then a two-path RSVP: I'm coming, or I can't make it.

(03)Visual direction

Night-drive darks, warm cream and one copper accent.

The intro and hero stay in the dark of the film. Agenda, details and RSVP move to warm cream, with the copper accent carried through both.

(05)Interactive features

  • Georgian first

    Georgian is the default and English is one tap away. Every label, date and form placeholder switches with it.

  • Music on the first tap

    The soundtrack starts only after the guest's first interaction once the intro has finished, with a visible play and pause control.

  • A skippable intro

    The film can be skipped at any moment. Visitors who ask for less motion see a single line instead of the full sequence.

  • A countdown in local time

    Days, hours, minutes and seconds to 28 June, 18:00, fixed to Tbilisi time whatever the guest's device clock says.

  • Two-path RSVP

    Accepting opens the form; declining is a single tap. Both are sent to the organisers' sheet, so a no is counted too.

  • A personal reference

    Each confirmation shows a reference in the form JETOUR·XXXX·2026, made from the guest's name.

(06)Technical execution

Build
Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step.
Motion
A GSAP timeline synced to the film for the intro, ScrollTrigger reveals and a reduced-motion path.
Media
Vertical H.264 film, muted and inline so it plays on phones; music as a separate track after the first interaction.
Typography
Orbitron, NormalidadCompact Bold, Rajdhani and Noto Sans Georgian.
RSVP data
Accept and decline replies posted to a Google Apps Script endpoint that writes to Google Sheets.
Hosting
GitHub Pages.

(07)Deliverables

  • Invitation website in Georgian and English
  • Intro sequence with timed figures
  • Live countdown and agenda
  • Dress code and recommendations
  • RSVP with accept and decline paths
  • Guest replies to Google Sheets
  • Responsive build and device testing
