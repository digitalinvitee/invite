Casa Gelso

A luxury fashion house opening in Tbilisi, introduced in three acts and three languages: an illustrated teaser, a brand reveal and a private invitation with RSVP.

Casa Gelso, Tbilisi
Sector
Multi-brand luxury fashion
Occasion

Languages

(01)Challenge

A new house had to be anticipated before it could be visited.

Casa Gelso brings Ferragamo, Moschino, Tom Ford and Bottega Veneta under one roof in Tbilisi. Before the doors opened, the launch had three jobs: build curiosity, explain the house's idea of accessible luxury, and invite guests personally, in Georgian, English and Italian.

(02)Concept

One story in three acts: tease, reveal, invite.

Instead of a teaser page, an ad and an invitation made separately, all three live in one site as consecutive acts. A guest who watched the teaser reaches the invitation already knowing the house.

  1. Act I · Teaser

    Something is coming.

    An illustrated film behind an Enter gate. Five frames and five lines, from “something to love” to “wear the moment”, resolving into a single date: 12.09.

  2. Act II · Brand reveal

    Accessible luxury.

    Chapter One turns into a scrolling digital ad in five beats, from “The fashion you dreamed of, now in your city” to “Come and see it in person”.

  3. Act III · Invitation

    You are invited.

    Date, time and address, an add-to-calendar link and an RSVP that asks each guest for their top three designers and one wish for the house.

(03)Visual direction

Wine, ivory and a hand-drawn line, from the brand book.

Everything starts from the Casa Gelso brand book: the monogram, wine and ivory, and a serif with an editorial voice. Watercolour illustration carries the teaser; product photography carries the reveal.

(04)Digital experience

Built to be watched first, then read, then answered.

On a laptop the site plays like a short film; on a phone it reads like a sequence of stories. Scroll to follow a guest through it.

  1. 01 · Gate

    Enter

    A dark screen and one word. Pressing Enter starts the film and its soundtrack, so sound only plays once a guest has chosen to begin. A sound toggle stays in the navigation.

  2. 02 · Teaser

    Something is coming

    Five illustrated frames, one line each, set like captions in a fashion film.

  3. 03 · Coming soon

    12.09

    The film resolves into the date, through a brief broken-signal cut.

  4. 04 · Brand reveal

    Accessible luxury

    Chapter One: the house's positioning in five short chapters, with the houses it carries.

  5. 05 · Invitation

    You are invited.

    Date, time and address, with a one-tap Google Calendar event. Every line exists in English, Georgian and Italian.

  6. 06 · RSVP

    Kindly confirm

    Attendance, top three designers and one wish for Casa Gelso. Replies go straight to the client's guest sheet.

(05)Interactive features

  • Sound by choice

    The soundtrack starts with the guest's own Enter, never on load, and a toggle in the navigation turns it off at any point.

  • Three languages

    English, Georgian and Italian, switchable at any moment. The chosen language is remembered on the next visit.

  • Add to calendar

    One tap creates a Google Calendar event with the date, time and address of the opening.

  • An RSVP with a wish

    Attendance, top three designers and a wish for the house, validated before sending, with a hidden honeypot field against bots.

  • Reduced motion

    Visitors who ask for less motion skip the film and smooth scrolling and land directly on the brand reveal.

  • Never a dead page

    If the animation libraries fail to load, a fallback unlocks the page and opens the full story as a readable document.

(06)Technical execution

Build
Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step.
Motion
GSAP and ScrollTrigger for the opener and chapters, Lenis for smooth scrolling, a CSS signal effect for the date.
Typography
Fraunces, DM Sans, Noto Serif Georgian and Noto Sans Georgian.
RSVP data
Posted to a Google Apps Script web app that writes each reply to the client's Google Sheet.
Accessibility
One h1, a skip link, labelled form controls, landmark structure and a reduced-motion path.
Hosting
GitHub Pages.

(07)Deliverables

  • Campaign concept and three-act narrative
  • Copy in English, Georgian and Italian
  • Art direction across film, ad and invitation
  • Sound for the opening sequence
  • Responsive campaign website
  • Invitation with calendar link
  • RSVP flow and guest sheet
  • Testing across devices and languages
