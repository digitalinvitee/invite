When you visit

Like every website, this one is delivered by a hosting provider: GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., USA (confirm, or name the host you use). To deliver each page, the host receives the technical information your browser sends with every request, such as your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser type, and may keep it in server logs for security and abuse prevention. We do not receive these logs and do not combine them with anything else.

Fonts, images, video and scripts are served from this website itself. Pages make no requests to font services, social networks, advertising networks or analytics services.

No cookies, no analytics

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools. It keeps two small settings in your own browser so the site behaves the way you left it:

NameWhat it doesHow long
invite.intro.seenRemembers that you have seen the opening sequence, so your next visit plays a shorter version.Until you clear this site's data
invite.soundRemembers whether you turned sound on or off.Until you clear this site's data

These values stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

When you contact us

If you use the contact form, we receive what you type: your name and email address and, for a project brief, the kind of project and your description, plus anything optional you choose to add (company or brand, timeline, budget range, notes). We also record the date and time you sent it. A “Just say hello” message is filed as a general enquiry.

We use this only to reply to you and, if you ask for it, to prepare a proposal. Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, the legal bases are Article 6(1)(b), steps taken at your request before a possible contract, and Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in answering messages. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell your message or pass it on to anyone for their own use.

Messages are delivered to us through Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet, services provided by Google (Google Privacy Policy), which process them on our behalf; we are also notified by email. Confirm the Google entity that provides your account (for example Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC) and the email provider that receives the notification.

If the optional spam check is switched on: when you start filling in the form, a security check from Cloudflare (Cloudflare Turnstile) loads and processes technical information about your browser and device to tell people from automated programs.

If you write to us by email or on WhatsApp instead, the same applies to what you send. WhatsApp messages are carried by WhatsApp, a Meta service, under its own terms (WhatsApp Privacy Policy).

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply and follow up. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted after period, for example 12 months. If we work together, project correspondence is kept for the length of the project and for as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask to receive it in a portable format. Write to privacy contact email and we will answer without undue delay.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Georgia, the Personal Data Protection Service; in the European Union, the authority in the country where you live or work.

Other websites

Case studies link to live client websites. Those sites are run separately and have their own privacy practices; this notice does not cover them.

Changes

If anything here changes, for example when a new service is added, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
