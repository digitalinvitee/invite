What it is

We design and produce digital advertising for brands, products and services, from animated banners and short motion ads to social creatives. Each campaign is built around your brand, your audience, the platform it runs on and the message it needs to carry.

Launch & Interactive Experiences

Build anticipation Build anticipationanticipation

Turn your next launch into an experience. From teasers and countdowns to interactive reveals, we build excitement before the big moment.

A launch worth waiting for.

Start a project like this →

What it is

We create connected experiences for the launch of a product, brand, service or campaign. They can stand alone as a microsite or combine into one story, designed and built in one place, from the first teaser to launch day.

When the launch also has a guest list, such as a private opening or a preview, the invitation and RSVP side is covered by 004 You’re Invited.

Why it works

Announcing a date is not enough. People need a reason to pay attention, a clear picture of what is coming and an easy way to take part. A planned experience builds interest step by step and gives the right audience one place to register.

What we can include

  • Launch microsites for products and brands
  • Teaser pages and campaigns
  • Countdown experiences
  • Interactive product reveals
  • Scroll-driven storytelling
  • Interactive launch presentations
  • Launch registration and inquiry flows
  • Connected launch campaigns, from teaser to launch day

004

Digital Invitations & Event Experiences

You’re invited You’re invitedinvited

Beautifully designed digital invitations for weddings, celebrations and corporate events — with everything your guests need in one easy-to-share link.

An invitation worth opening.

Start a project like this →

What it is

From weddings and birthdays to private and corporate events, we create interactive digital invitations that make every occasion feel special — and keep everything guests need in one place.

Why it works

One beautiful link brings together the invitation, event details, maps and RSVP. Guests know where to go and how to respond, while you keep track of replies without endless messages.

What we can include

  • Custom invitation design
  • Interactive animations
  • Event schedule
  • Location and maps
  • Dress code
  • Countdown
  • RSVP
  • Guest response tracking

Where feasible, an invitation can be personalized for each guest or group. We collect only the details the event needs, and agree with you where replies are stored and who can see them.

(04)How we work

From first conversation to final delivery.

A clear, collaborative process tailored to your project — with defined steps, feedback and no unnecessary complications.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with your idea, your audience, your objectives and any constraints, so the creative work has something solid to answer.

    You get
    A clear project brief and agreed direction.
  2. 02

    Concept

    We develop the creative direction, the structure of the content and how people will interact with it.

    You get
    The concept, key visuals and an interaction plan.
  3. 03

    Design & Build

    The visuals, the motion and the working experience are made in one place, and built to work on every screen.

    You get
    A staging link to click through and comment on.
  4. 04

    Refine & Deliver

    We test it, improve it, finalize it and prepare it for launch.

    You get
    A tested, finished experience, ready to go live.

(05)The studio

The idea you approve is the idea that ships.

INVITÉ is an independent creative digital studio in Tbilisi, working with clients worldwide. It was founded by Mariam Kajaia, a front-end developer focused on creative web development, interactive experiences and digital design.

We combine art direction and technology with a clear understanding of what each project is for. Design should do more than look beautiful: it should attract attention, communicate clearly and make the next step easier.

  • One person, from concept to code.

    The person who shapes the concept also writes the code, so nothing is lost between what you approve and what ships.

  • Clear scopes, dates and sign-offs.

    Before INVITÉ, Mariam spent four years managing corporate client accounts, so projects here run on a defined scope, agreed dates and sign-offs.

  • Anything extra is agreed at the start.

    If a brief needs more than design and code, such as photography, film or translation, we say so at the start and plan it into the scope.
